Privacy

Billbook is built so your invoices stay yours.

What stays on your device

Your business details, clients, drafts and saved invoices are stored in your browser’s local storage. They are not uploaded to our servers, and we cannot see them. Clearing your browser data deletes them — use the Backup feature in Pro if you need a copy.

What we collect

We may use privacy-friendly, aggregate analytics (page views, no invoice content) to understand how the site is used. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts and the QR library from cdnjs, which may receive your IP address as part of normal web requests.

Payments

Pro purchases are handled by our payment provider. We do not see or store your card details.

Not tax or legal advice

Billbook helps you produce invoice documents. Tax rules differ by country and change over time. Please confirm requirements with a qualified professional.
